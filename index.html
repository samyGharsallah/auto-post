auto-post

A personal desktop tool for publishing my own short videos.

What it does

auto-post is a command-line tool that runs on my own computer. I use it to publish short videos I produce myself to social accounts that I own and have explicitly authorized. It reads the video files and their captions from a folder on my machine and uploads them, so I do not have to upload each video by hand.

How it works

  • The tool runs locally. There is no server, no website and no hosted service behind it.
  • I select an account, then a video, review the caption, and confirm the upload.
  • Nothing is ever uploaded without an explicit confirmation on my part.
  • Access tokens are stored in files on my own computer and are never transmitted anywhere except to the platform that issued them.

Who it is for

It is a single-user tool for my own accounts. It does not post on behalf of other people, it has no user accounts of its own, and it is not offered as a product or a service to anyone else.

Supported platforms

YouTube, Facebook Pages and TikTok.
